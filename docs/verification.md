# Verification checklist

For the product owner. Every spec below is built, tested and on both branches ("Ported"). Verifying one means:

1. **In the app:** open the screen named under *Where to look* and try what the spec's rules say.
2. **Against your Jira** (only where the last column says so): some rules describe Jira from documentation or product knowledge, so they need a look at your Jira DC. That part is done on the system with your Jira, together with the inventory (spec 0801).
3. **Then** change the spec's status line and its README row to `Verified (PO, date)`.

Done automatically on 12 Nov 2026: every rule of every spec is cited by a test, or its acceptance table says why not (`python3 scripts/spec-coverage.py .`). All suites pass on both branches.
